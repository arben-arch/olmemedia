New to social media?

Why does my business need this?

Many people look up a business on their phone before they visit. An active page shows them you are open and what you offer.

What is a post? What is an ad?

A post is a video or image on your page. Publishing is free. Making good ones every week takes time, and that is the part we do for you.

An ad is when you pay Facebook or Instagram to show your video to more people, such as people who live near you. You choose how much to spend, and you can stop at any time. For example, 5 euro a day is about 150 euro a month, paid by you directly to Facebook.

What does “run by us” mean?

We choose who sees the ad, set it up, check how it is doing and change what does not work. The ad money goes straight from you to Facebook, not to us.

What is in the monthly report?

One page in plain words: how many people saw your posts, which ones did best, and what we plan next. With the Ads package, also what the ad money was spent on and what it brought.

What do I have to do?

Tell us your offers and news, send photos if you have them, and give us access to post. We do the rest.
