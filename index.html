New to social media? Here it is in plain words.

You do not need to know how any of this works. That is our job.

Why does my business need this?

Many people look up a business on their phone before they visit. An active page with good videos shows them you are open, what you offer and what it is like to be there.

What is a post?

A video or image on your Instagram, TikTok or Facebook page. Publishing is free. Making good ones every week takes time, and that is the part we do for you.

What is an ad?

You pay Facebook or Instagram to show your video to more people, such as people who live near your business. You choose how much to spend, and you can stop at any time.

What is ads management?

Ads need setting up and watching. We choose who sees the ad, set it up, check how it is doing and change what does not work. The ad money goes straight from you to Facebook, not to us.

What is in the monthly report?

One page in plain words: how many people saw your posts, which ones did best, and what we plan next. With the Ads package, also what the ad money was spent on and what it brought.

What do I have to do?

Tell us your offers and news, send photos if you have them, and give us access to post. We do the rest. You keep running your business.

Posts and ads: the difference

Posts

  • Cost: free to publish
  • Who sees them: the app decides, often people who already follow you
  • Good for: keeping your page active and giving people a reason to trust you

Ads

  • Cost: you choose a budget
  • Who sees them: the people we choose, such as those who live near you
  • Good for: reaching people who have not heard of you yet
